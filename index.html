Experience

Design Build Fly (DBF)

Aerodynamics Team

Contributed to UCSD’s Design-Build-Fly effort by supporting aerodynamic configuration decisions and practical build-to-test iteration on a student RC aircraft. Performed airfoil screening and low-fidelity analysis (XFOIL/XFLR5) to compare lift/drag trends under Reynolds-number constraints typical of competition-scale aircraft, and used results to inform geometry choices and stability targets. Helped translate analysis into CAD updates and manufacturing-ready geometry, then supported flight readiness through preflight checks and post-flight review. Worked with onboard telemetry limited to battery voltage and link/signal strength to improve operational reliability and reduce “unknowns” during test days.

Aspirations

My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of aerodynamic performance, propulsion integration, and system-level aircraft design — building the next generation of ultra-efficient commercial aircraft. I’m especially motivated by unconventional configurations (like blended wing bodies) and by design decisions grounded in disciplined trade studies, simulation, and test-informed iteration.


I’m exploring Master’s programs in Europe with a focus aligned to flight performance and power, while keeping a strong interest in the broader space ecosystem and advanced vehicle development. As an Argentine and Italian citizen and fluent in English, Spanish, and French, I thrive in international technical teams — and I want my career to be defined by high-impact engineering work that connects clean performance to real-world operability.
